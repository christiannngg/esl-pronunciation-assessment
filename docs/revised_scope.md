# Revised scope and decision log (US 7.2)

**Status:** draft for review, 2026-10-29. Evidence: `docs/midterm_results_report.md` (US 7.1). Calendar and stories: `docs/go_forward_plan.md`.

**Bottom line.** Keep the full scope: all 9 tasks (7 SpeechOcean762 outputs, 2 L2-ARCTIC outputs) stay in. Nothing in the results argues for dropping a head, and the calendar leaves roughly three weeks of slack. What changes is the _claims_ and three details of Sprints 8-9, listed below.

## 1. Is the original scope still realistic?

**Technical risk (from the results).**

| output                                         | state at midterm                                                                                 | decision                                                                |
| ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| sentence accuracy / fluency / prosodic / total | fine-tuned clearly beats baseline; frozen already close; multi-task at parity                    | keep                                                                    |
| word accuracy                                  | weakest task; low scores are compressed toward the majority score (W8)                           | keep, report as a known limitation; optional low-score loss in Sprint 8 |
| phone score                                    | strong gain over baseline; multi-task at parity                                                  | keep                                                                    |
| phone mispronunciation                         | large gain over baseline; the one head hurt by plain multi-task (-0.026 F1); GradNorm repairs it | keep with `MT-GN15`                                                     |
| L2 phone error / word error                    | large gain over baseline; multi-task about equal to fine-tuned                                   | keep                                                                    |

The proposal's own fallback ("reduce the number of prediction heads while preserving sentence-, word-, and phoneme-level assessment") is not triggered. It stays on record as the contingency if Sprint 8 or 9 slips.

**Schedule risk.** The roadmap's 10 one-week sprints end in week 11 (about Nov 22). The final report is due Mon Dec 14, so there are about three spare weeks (Nov 23 - Dec 13, with Thanksgiving on Nov 26). Heavy compute is not the bottleneck: US 5.3 timings put one fine-tuning job at about 2-3 minutes on a shared GPU (indicative only), so even a 20-job experiment is under an hour of GPU time. The costly parts are analysis, the app and writing.

## 3. What changes in the roadmap

- **US 8.1:** drop the "fully unseen speakers" wording; evaluate the carried systems (single-task reference, `MT-GN15`, `MT-full`) by L1 background and age group with speaker-level intervals. The significance tests against the baseline are already done in US 7.1.
- **US 8.2:** the ablation table can reuse the existing single-task and multi-task runs (frozen vs fine-tuned and single- vs multi-task need no new training).
- **US 8.3:** one subgroup, one fix (D4).
- **US 9.1:** the app serves `MT-GN15` plus the L2 model (D6). **Risk to check first:** phone and word feedback needs forced alignment at inference time, and the training pipeline used alignment tooling. Confirm that tooling can run inside the backend before building the front end.
- **Weekly progress videos** are added to every remaining sprint, starting week 9.
- **Buffer weeks** are named in the plan so they are used on purpose (report, rehearsal, reruns), not absorbed by earlier sprints.

## 4. What does not change

The research questions stay as written in the proposal: pretrained vs traditional (answered: yes), multi-task vs independent (answered: parity), generalisation to unseen speakers (answered on the speaker-held-out test set now, broken down in Sprint 8). Dataset choices, metrics, speaker-independent splits and the weakness-then-fix structure are unchanged.
